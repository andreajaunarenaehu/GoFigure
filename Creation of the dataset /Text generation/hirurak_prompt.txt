You are tasked with generating metaphors and their paraphrases based on a given source and target. Your goal is to create 10 textual metaphors, along with their literal paraphrases and contradiction paraphrases.

You will be provided with two input variables:
<source>
{{SOURCE}}
</source>

<target>
{{TARGET}}
</target>

The source represents the conceptual domain from which you will draw your metaphorical expressions, while the target represents the concept or idea you are trying to describe metaphorically.

Follow these steps to complete the task:

1. Generate 10 unique textual metaphors that relate the source to the target. Each metaphor should be creative and illustrative of some aspect of the target in terms of the source.

2. For each metaphor, create a literal paraphrase that expresses the same idea without using figurative language. The literal paraphrase should clearly explain the meaning of the metaphor in plain terms.

3. For each metaphor, also create a contradiction paraphrase. This should express an idea that contradicts or is opposite to the original metaphor's meaning.

Present your output in the following format:

<metaphors>
<metaphor_set>
<metaphor>
[Insert metaphor here]
</metaphor>
<literal_paraphrase>
[Insert literal paraphrase here]
</literal_paraphrase>
<contradiction_paraphrase>
[Insert contradiction paraphrase here]
</contradiction_paraphrase>
</metaphor_set>
[Repeat this structure for all 10 metaphors]
</metaphors>

Here's an example of how your output should be structured (using "time" as the source and "money" as the target):

<metaphors>
<metaphor_set>
<metaphor>
Time is a valuable currency.
</metaphor>
<literal_paraphrase>
Time is a limited and important resource that should be used wisely.
</literal_paraphrase>
<contradiction_paraphrase>
Time is an infinite and worthless commodity that can be wasted freely.
</contradiction_paraphrase>
</metaphor_set>
[The remaining 9 metaphor sets would follow in the same format]
</metaphors>

Remember to create 10 unique and creative metaphors, each with its corresponding literal and contradiction paraphrases. Ensure that your metaphors effectively relate the source to the target in various ways.
